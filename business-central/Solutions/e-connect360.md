# e-Connect360
> Update: 14.08.2026

Integrated business communication and data exchange platform for Dynamics 365 Business Central

**The e-Connect360** application is a comprehensive integration platform that extends Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central with powerful tools for electronic communication, EDI integration, document exchange, and data synchronization.
Designed for organizations that need to connect Business Central with customers, suppliers, partners, and external systems, e-Connect360 provides a scalable and reliable framework for automating business processes, eliminating manual data entry, and ensuring seamless information flow across the enterprise. 
Whether you need to exchange electronic documents, automate EDI communication, synchronize master data between systems, or integrate Business Central with third-party applications, e-Connect360 delivers everything in a single solution.
